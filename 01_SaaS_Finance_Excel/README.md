# 💼 Financial & Risk Performance Dashboard

## 🎯 The Business Problem
A multi-sector enterprise was struggling to track their operational efficiency and liquidity across various divisions (Healthcare, IT Services, Retail, Logistics, etc.). The finance team spent days manually consolidating spreadsheets to compare Revenue against Operational Expenses (OPEX). Furthermore, leadership lacked real-time visibility into outstanding invoices (Receivable Days) and upcoming cash flow risks.

## 🛠️ The Technical Solution
I engineered a fully automated, interactive financial model in Excel:
* **Data Architecture:** Connected raw transactional data to a hidden backend calculation engine, keeping the front-end UI clean and secure.
* **Dynamic Visuals:** Designed a clear, clustered column chart to track monthly Revenue vs. OPEX trends across a two-year span (2024–2025).
* **Interactive Controls:** Implemented Slicers allowing executives to instantly filter financial performance by specific Years and Sectors.
* **Custom KPIs:** Created highly visible metric cards for Total Revenue, OPEX, Receivable Days, and an algorithmic "Next Month Cashflow Risk" indicator.

## 📈 The Business Impact
* **Instant Liquidity Tracking:** Leadership can instantly see that average Receivable Days sit at 39, allowing them to adjust collection policies proactively.
* **Risk Mitigation:** The dedicated "Next Month Cashflow Risk" metric serves as an early-warning system for the CFO to prevent operational cash shortages.
* **Zero Manual Effort:** Because the dashboard is built on a structured automated model, updating the report for the next month takes seconds (a simple "Refresh All" click) instead of hours of manual copy-pasting.
